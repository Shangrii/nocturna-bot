<objective>
Build the durable storage layer for meetings and generalize the action-queue dedupe from
"global by kind" to "per-entity by dedupe_key" — the one and only shared-infrastructure change
this phase makes. This is the foundation both the bot cog (persist/re-publish) and the FastAPI
router (browse/enqueue) build on.

Purpose: MEET-01 needs a persisted meeting record that survives a bot restart; MEET-03's
"never duplicate even on double-click/retry" guarantee needs correct per-meeting dedupe (the
current kind-only dedupe would misroute two Managers republishing two different meetings —
RESEARCH.md Pitfall 1).
Output: New `meetings` table + CRUD in `core/db.py`; backward-compatible `dedupe_key` column +
`enqueue_deduped` signature extension in `core/action_queue.py`; RED-first tests.
</objective>
